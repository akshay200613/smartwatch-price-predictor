Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our smartwatch valuation model

How accurate is the Random Forest price prediction?
Our model achieves an 87.4% R² Score on cross-validated test splits, accurately predicting the actual real-world transactional selling price within an average margin of ±8-12%.
Why do Apple and Garmin hold significantly higher value?
Apple and Garmin command premium secondary market prices due to proprietary software ecosystems, multi-year OS update lifecycles, and high buyer demand that resists steep brand depreciation.
How do ratings and review volumes impact valuation?
Higher ratings (>4.3) with significant review counts (>2,000) serve as strong trust signals, reducing perceived buyer risk and bolstering market price retention by up to 10-15%.
